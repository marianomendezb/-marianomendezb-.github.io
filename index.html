Hello!

I'm Mariano, composer from Argentina, writing and producing original bespoke music, sound design and audio post-production for motion design, advertising, animation, games and film.

Since 2014 I've partnered with agencies and studios worldwide to create original music & sounds for brands like Nike, Netflix, Hulu, MTV and ONU, while also collaborating closely with artists and friends on experimental & non-commercial projects.

I co-founded and directed Puerto, a creative audio content studio that focused on the development of musical ideas for audiovisual projects from 2017 to 2022.

Since 2023 I work remotely from my studio based in Buenos Aires, where I also work as a producer and session musician, recording live drums and guitars, producing records, mixing and mastering music for artists across the globe.

I'm currently working on generative music, audio design and implementation for games, diving deep into it.
