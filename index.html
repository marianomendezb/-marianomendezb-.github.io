Hello!

I'm Mariano, composer from Argentina, writing and producing original bespoke music, sound design and audio post-production for motion design, advertising, animation and film.

Since 2014 I've partnered with agencies and studios worldwide to create original music & sounds for brands like Nike, Netflix, Hulu, MTV and ONU, while also collaborating closely with artists and friends on experimental & non-commercial projects.

From 2017 to 2022 I co-founded and directed Puerto, a creative audio content studio that focused on the development of musical ideas for audiovisual projects.

In 2023 I started working remotely from my studio based in Buenos Aires, where I also work as a producer and session musician, recording live drums and guitars, composing, arranging and producing full songs and records, while also doing mixing and mastering work for artists and musicians worldwide.

My side music project is Salvador Rivera, a band born in 2012. Currently working on generative music for games.

Let's make noise together!
