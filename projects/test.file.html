Test.file

Client: Ezequiel Torres, Espacio El Molino
Work: Sound design, audio post
Info: To carry out this animated project, several artists gathered at Espacio El Molino, located in Villa Giardino, Córdoba, Argentina. Our aim was to create a collaborative and experimental animation. Out of these meetings emerged Test.file, where each participant contributed their aesthetic sense and work to animate their fragments.
This is the Director's Cut.
Mariano Méndez — Buenos Aires
