Afterglow

Client: Netflix
Work: Bespoke music, sound design, audio post
Info: Produced and designed by Neko Studio, they created a bold and unique look inspired by classic TV bars and tones, but with a fresh, modern twist. Blending nostalgic references with contemporary design, they crafted an aesthetic that feels both playful and current, setting the perfect tone for the show's conversations and recaps. The result is a striking visual world that reflects the spirit of the series. To highlight these features I composed music blending modern electronic beats with vintage sythesizers providing movement and texture to the piece. The melody was developed from some classic TV bar and tones sounds. Finally extra sound design was added to complete the work.
Mariano Méndez — Buenos Aires
